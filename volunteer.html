Find Your Place at NEX

Whether you enjoy planning events, creating content, designing visuals, or supporting participants, there is room to contribute and learn alongside the team.

  • Session and logistics support
  • Team communication

  • Partnership research

  • Posters and announcements
  • Canva or other design tools

Marketing & Social Media

  • Content calendars and captions
  • Campaign ideas

Why Volunteer with NEX?

Contribute to a growing student-led initiative while developing practical skills through meaningful collaboration.

🌱Build real-world skills
🧭Develop leadership and teamwork
💬Practice communication
🪄Explore creative ideas
🌍Support youth-led opportunities
📜Certificate subject to participation and NEX policy

Who Can Apply?

We welcome enthusiastic students who are curious, dependable, respectful, and willing to learn. Previous debate or MUN experience is helpful for some roles, but it is not required for every team.

✨ Enthusiasm🤝 Teamwork🗓️ Reliability 💡 Creativity🗣️ Clear communication🌱 Willingness to learn 💜 Respectful collaboration
